Scope and methodology

The audit covered the reserve account backing the bridge between 1 January and 30 June 2026. Balances were sampled daily from the chain and reconciled against custodian statements. The methodology section is deliberately long so that the interesting sentence does not sit in the first few hundred characters of the document.

Sampling notes: daily snapshots, custodian confirmations, and a quarterly reconciliation with the operator's own reporting. No exceptions were raised during reconciliation. Cookie notice: this fixture sets no cookies. Related reports: 2025 audit, 2024 audit, custody review.

Further methodology: the reserve is held in a single custody account; the auditors verified signing thresholds, reviewed withdrawal approvals, and confirmed that no withdrawal exceeded the documented limits during the period under review.

Findings

The 2026 reserve audit reports 12,400 ETH held in the bridge reserve as of 30 June 2026. The reserve fully covered outstanding bridged balances on every sampled day.
