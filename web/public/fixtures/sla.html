Vendor service level agreement

This page is a fixture used as dispute evidence in the Evidence Desk demo. It is a file in this repository, so every change an on-chain case reacts to is a public commit anyone can diff.

Scope

The agreement covers the hosted API and the status page. Operational detail is deliberately verbose here so the clause under dispute does not sit in the first few hundred characters of the document: contact routes, escalation order, the support rota, maintenance windows, and the review cadence for this page. None of that is the commitment itself.

Maintenance windows are announced at least five working days ahead and do not count against availability. Planned migrations follow the same notice period. Support hours are 09:00-18:00 CET on working days, with an on-call rotation outside them.

Last reviewed: 3 October 2026. Contact routes updated; commitments unchanged.

Commitments

The vendor promises 99.9% monthly uptime, and service credits are paid within 30 days of an accepted claim. Claims are filed through support with the incident reference from the status page.

Claims procedure

File within 30 days of the incident, include the incident reference, and the vendor responds within ten working days. Rejected claims may be escalated once.
